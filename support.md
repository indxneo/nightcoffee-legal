# Night Coffee Support

Night Coffee is a story game for iPhone: seven nights behind the counter at Nighthawks, a 24-hour coffee counter in Wicker Park, Chicago, from December 25 to 31, 1999. You make the drinks by hand and listen to the regulars.

## Getting help

Found a problem, or have a question? Email [alishersv.dev@outlook.com](mailto:alishersv.dev@outlook.com) and tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened. A screenshot helps. If you have a GitHub account, you can also [open an issue](https://github.com/indxneo/nightcoffee-legal/issues).

## Common questions

**What's free, and what does the purchase unlock?** Night 1 is free. One purchase, The Whole Week, unlocks Nights 2 to 7. There are no ads and nothing else to buy.

**I bought the week. How do I get it back on a new iPhone, or after reinstalling?** Sign in with the same Apple Account, then go to Settings, the Play tab, and tap Restore purchase. The same button is on the purchase screen.

**It says "Waiting for the App Store…"** The purchase is waiting for approval, for example from a parent through Ask to Buy. The week opens by itself once it's approved.

**Can I get a refund?** Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us: go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Does the game save?** Yes, automatically, at each visit and at the end of each night. Tap Continue on the title screen to pick up where you left off.

**Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?** Yes. Your progress and chapters are kept only on your iPhone, so deleting the app erases them (restoring your whole iPhone from a backup that includes the app brings them back). Your purchase isn't lost: Restore purchase brings it back.

**Can I play a night again?** Yes. Once you reach Night 2, Chapters on the title screen replays any night you've reached.

**How do I make the drinks?** The first time each tool appears, a How to play card explains it. To read it again during play, tap Menu, then How to play. If you'd rather just enjoy the story, turn on Easy brewing in Settings, the Play tab.

**What's in Settings?** Reading: language (English or Russian), text size, text speed and auto-advance. Sound: music, radio sound, sound effects and vibration. Play: Easy brewing, Reduce motion, Solid panels and Restore purchase. Settings is on the title screen, and under Menu during play.

**What does the game need?** An iPhone with iOS 16 or later. The game plays in landscape, and works fully offline.

**Is there anything I should know before playing?** The story includes grief and loss, handled gently.

**Does the game collect my data?** No. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/).

# Night Coffee Support

Night Coffee is a story game for iPhone: seven nights behind the counter at Nighthawks, a 24-hour coffee counter in Wicker Park, Chicago, from December 25 to 31, 1999. You make the drinks by hand and listen to the regulars.

## Getting help

Found a problem, or have a question? Email [alishersv.dev@outlook.com](mailto:alishersv.dev@outlook.com) and tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened. A screenshot helps. If you have a GitHub account, you can also [open an issue](https://github.com/indxneo/nightcoffee-legal/issues).

## Common questions

**Does the game save?** Yes, automatically, at each visit and at the end of each night. Tap Continue on the title screen to pick up where you left off.

**Can I change how the text looks?** Yes. Settings has the text size, text speed and auto-advance, plus Easy brewing, Reduce motion, vibration, music and sound volumes, and the language (English or Russian).

**What does the game need?** An iPhone with iOS 16 or later. The game plays in landscape.

**Is there anything I should know before playing?** The story includes grief and loss, handled gently.

**Does the game collect my data?** No. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/).

# Night Coffee Privacy Policy

**Effective October 26, 2026**

Night Coffee is a story game for iPhone made by Alisher Serikbayev, who publishes as indxneo ("we" below).

**The short version: Night Coffee doesn't collect any information about you.** It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send anything over the internet itself. The one purchase goes through the App Store on your iPhone.

## What stays on your iPhone

The game saves your progress (the night you're on and the choices you've made), your settings (such as text size, sound and language) and the radio's state in the app's own storage on your iPhone. None of it leaves your iPhone through Night Coffee, and we can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Like other apps' data, iOS may include it in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud or a computer). Those backups are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

## What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, files, device identifiers, usage data or crash data. Night Coffee never asks for access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

## Purchases

Night 1 is free, and one purchase in the app unlocks the rest of the week. Apple handles the payment through the App Store; we never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only asks your iPhone's App Store whether the purchase was made (when you buy, when you tap Restore, and when the game starts), and keeps a note on your iPhone that the week is unlocked.

## Apple's services

Apple runs the App Store and TestFlight and may collect information when you use them, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, or send feedback or crash reports through TestFlight, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports and usage totals, or your feedback, to us. We use them only to fix and improve the game.

## Children

Night Coffee isn't directed at children under 13. It collects no information from anyone, children included.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Every change is public in this page's [history](https://github.com/indxneo/nightcoffee-legal/commits/main/privacy.md).
